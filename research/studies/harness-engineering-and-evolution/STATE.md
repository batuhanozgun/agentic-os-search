## Current bounded finding

The video frames operational responsibilities; the paper searches over executable harness policies. Their useful intersection is the transition from observable work history to a candidate system change. They do not establish that every agent failure is a harness failure, that more components are always better, or that a higher benchmark score qualifies a change for SOUL.

Three distinctions must survive continuation:

1. Running harness ≠ harness-search procedure ≠ authority accepting a change.
2. Available history ≠ faithfully selected evidence ≠ valid diagnosis ≠ demonstrated improvement.
3. Benchmark-specific discovery ≠ held-out task/model transfer ≠ safe operational adoption.
