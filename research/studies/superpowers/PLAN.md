### v0.7 — U06-A refinement

- Split U06 because the evaluator is a separately versioned development dependency, not more hidden files in the primary plugin. Authoring/admission has a bounded answer; evaluator implementation and release qualification do not yet.
- Treat intervention form as a research variable: automation versus reusable guidance; prohibition versus positive recipe; required slot versus reminder; observable conditional versus vague exception.
- Keep metadata discovery, full-body use and behavioral effect distinct. Historical anecdotes inside current files do not override current task-review topology.
- Distinguish no-guidance benefit from improvement over an existing revision. Repeated same-scenario tuning is useful development evidence, not independent generalization proof.
- Separate stimuli, expected outcomes, historical narratives, runtime traces and final verdicts. Do not convert a worked test design or a creation-log claim into a completed experiment.
- Human contribution review and an evaluation checklist are admission duties, not automatically enforced CI or evidence of released behavior.
- Record the newer lab pin without silently rebinding the primary target or claiming compatibility. Treat broad-permission live evals as a separate, unauthorized execution class.
- No ceremonial experiment: this unit produced source findings without inventing a new test or pass score. No earlier criterion or closure condition was relaxed.
