## What U08-A resolves and preserves

The native model explains two linked loops: doing development work and changing/evaluating/distributing the method. It locates semantic coordination in the controller without claiming that every error has one root cause. It keeps preparation, authority, review standing, actual effects and surviving evidence separate.

Some apparent tensions resolve by route, actor, resource or actual call path: three request paths; serial SDD versus independent-domain parallelism; one reviewer/two verdicts; recorded task BASE versus a generic example; native discovery versus a universal injector account; and inner scratch versus outer worktree retention. Q13/Q20/Q24 retain real unresolved scope/authority/freshness composition limits. Plausible reconciliation is not source-defined precedence.

The I01/I02 discussion challenges the assumption that less preparation ceremony automatically reduces controller context or improves work. The reporter supplies no transcript and expressly does not claim bad work in the follow-up. Host-policy explanations remain unverified. New Q47 concerns the separate dimensions of ceremony, context isolation, verification effort and decision authority, not an authorized live benchmark.
