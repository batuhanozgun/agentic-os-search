## Purpose and primary question

The user authorized detailed studies research with a gstack-like source/synthesis methodology and successive continuation. The objective is a source-grounded explanation and reusable candidate evidence for later SOUL / Development OS work, not installation or adoption.

How do shared procedures, host delivery, work artifacts, actor decisions, verification, lifecycle and skill evolution compose from a request to delivered work, and where do their guarantees stop?

Upstream instructions remain research objects, not active agent-control. Installed, discoverable, delivered, selected, followed and effective differ. So do reviewed/tested/integrated state, accepted residual risk, retained evidence, configured/materialized/loaded treatment, valid report and sound judgment. Visual access, interaction, selected state and approval are likewise distinct.

## Evidence and authorization boundaries

Candidate research is not Foundation truth or SOUL / Development OS architecture. No user-host installation, upstream issue/change, credentialed live-model campaign or adoption is authorized.

U06 source controls and public first-party reports are not our live execution. D30/D31 are full historical reports; D37 is a selected later record. Private transcripts/receipts were not retrieved or authenticated. Reported disagreements remain attributed, not general error rates or independently adjudicated private runs.

U07-A executed only the exact helper in a simulated client. All nine expected observations completed; two stronger criteria were unmet. Successful runner exit is not product qualification. Source fidelity, useful fixture controls and direct code reasoning do not establish actual host behavior, universal security, real user approval or production event loss.

A complete schema and read reference do not prove sound judgment; a result digest proves bytes, not truth. Representation repair can improve accepted-result counts without improving the subject. Pricing known usage does not establish total coverage. Static/unit CI is not live efficacy or actual release admission. Similarly, a shared endpoint/key need not restore a prior screen, and retaining HTML need not archive its interaction history.

The source contains meaningful protections and native tradeoffs. Its limits must not become either blanket unreliability or universal guarantees. U06 and U07-A close only at their explanatory/source/local scopes.
