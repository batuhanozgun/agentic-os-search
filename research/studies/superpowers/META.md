## Current bounded results

**U01:** selected shell, native-description, declared-context/session-start and OpenCode/Pi/Hermes delivery arrangements; different cache/re-entry/failure and tool-mapping semantics. Not full supported-host qualification.

**U02:** phase-conditioned authority, spec/plan/global/interface contracts, host-owned workspace selection, ledger recovery and task extraction. Plan path is not revision; saved plan is not necessarily materialized in a new worktree; controller transfers the necessary globals and prior rulings.

**U03:** one task reviewer returns two verdicts; scoped re-review and broad final review have distinct coverage. DONE initiates review, unverified requirements return to the controller, and completion can include parked findings with rulings. Context and cost controls do not establish independent execution or full assurance. Parallel-dispatch and serial SDD operate under different scope assumptions. Generic/specialized review instructions retain unresolved authority/range seams.

Four bounded local experiments are preserved:

- U01-A exact shell script: four transport observations, two failed health criteria, one negative control.
- U01-B exact OpenCode module: eight mechanism observations, two failed semantic-health criteria, one negative control.
- U02 exact workspace/task-brief helpers and literal ignore command: eleven expected observations, two failed health criteria; basename collision, unchanged ledger after plan revision, materialization and selected-path limits.
- U03 exact review-package helper with explicit OUTFILE plus a literal description-test predicate: seven expected helper/scope/control observations, two failed oracle-alignment criteria. Correct BASE retained multi-commit changes; a narrower valid range omitted an earlier file; dirty working state did not change a fixed-commit packet. Constructed by-file/direct-paste answers exposed one predicate's misalignment, not an actual model or CI failure.
