## Competing readings retained

ECC is not merely a board: the inspected path provisions worktrees and sends executable commands. Those effects are not an accepted-result scheduler. A process-completed label may be intentionally narrower than task success; a consumer must not silently broaden it. Raw handoff data may remain recoverable even when a derived parser view omits it. Created-resource rollback is useful but does not itself distinguish valuable later outputs from disposable setup. Other controllers may supply additional guarantees; one inspected path does not prove global absence.

The previous alternatives remain: local boards may intentionally allow reassignment/reopening; source refresh may intentionally replace a view; identical bytes do not confer ownership; bridge records may support cleanup/safe refusal without transparent retry. Keep policies scoped to their actual entrypoint.
