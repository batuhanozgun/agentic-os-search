## Interpretations and reopening

One Node evaluator, logger, worker or Rust store does not represent all ECC. An unavailable executor can be deliberate protection. Native review preserves uncertainty without proving its judgments. Queued work, local logs, archival memory, heuristic learning, derived boards and cooperative review may be useful without satisfying stronger authority/acceptance guarantees.

Notes13–15 preserve positive controls while explaining their scope. The state guard really rejects some late transitions, but an old finalizer can issue a currently legal transition after a same-id replacement. Child-handle drop, task-handle drop, future cancellation, runtime shutdown and persisted failure are not interchangeable. Actual session schema/writers and declared-version dependency source support that explanation; no live orphan/race or rate was observed.

Q04g1c retains the stronger uncertainty rather than making it an endless prerequisite for source closure. New caller/source/schema/host/configuration or authority evidence can reopen dependent findings. Rust scheduling/delegation/remote/evaluation and broad TUI features remain separate material frontiers, not completed by Q04g1.

After actual publication closure continue U09-B/Q04g2, not another reading of the answered Rust lifecycle. Do not pool E07 with older experiments or call source schedules E08 results.
