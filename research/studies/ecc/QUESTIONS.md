## Preserved competing readings

Different entrypoints can share helpers without sharing authority. Local boards can intentionally allow blocked overrides, reassignment and reopening. Such flexibility is not an exclusive claim/lease guarantee. Source refresh can intentionally replace a local view, but imported authorship and execution assignment remain different concepts. A snapshot can be saved outside its eventual reader's store; a recognized injected writer can bridge it. One reader's omission does not prove no integration elsewhere. Readiness summaries and refusal checks have value without proving task-level safety or benefit.
