## Interpretations and reopening

One Node evaluator, logger, worker or Rust store does not represent all ECC. An unavailable executor can be deliberate protection. Native review preserves uncertainty without proving its judgments. Queued work, local logs, archival memory, heuristic learning, derived boards and cooperative review may be useful without satisfying stronger authority/acceptance guarantees.

Notes13–14 establish positive implementation mechanisms alongside boundaries. A failed method can leave earlier SQL effects; a sequential transition guard is not necessarily atomic admission; a directory may exist without its former Git identity; an operator view may also execute control. E07 supports only the prescribed SQL/Git contrasts, not a measured ECC race, process orphan, live data-loss incident or restored model conversation. Separate producer/schema/host/configuration/authority changes can reopen dependent findings.
