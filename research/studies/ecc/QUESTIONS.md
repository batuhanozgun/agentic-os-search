## Preserved alternatives

ECC may derive much of its behavior from hosts; distinct entrypoints may still share important helpers; coarse module selection can be intentional; registration checks can be valid yet narrower than useful readiness. A clean synthesis can still omit a material runtime. Keep these possibilities explicit rather than turning either praise or defect discovery into the research objective.

## Reopening and status rules

Keep stable IDs. Split questions point to their children; superseded findings remain recoverable. A new source version, changed consumer, unexamined failure branch or loss of a necessary distinction can reopen a bounded answer. OPEN, ACTIVE, BOUNDED-ANSWERED, BLOCKED, DEFERRED, REOPENED and SUPERSEDED have distinct meanings. An unresolved risk documented in a table is not thereby resolved.
