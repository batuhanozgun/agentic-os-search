## Interpretations and reopening

One Node evaluator, logger, worker or Rust store does not represent all ECC. An unavailable candidate executor can be deliberate protection; it does not prohibit a different trusted record-mode tool route. Native review preserves uncertainty without proving its judgments. Queued work, archival memory, learning proposals, derived boards and cooperative review may be useful without stronger authority/acceptance guarantees.

Notes13–15 preserve real state guards and conditional ownership boundaries. Note16 joins actual Node/Rust targets without assuming one controller. Note17 explains transactional recorded-score admission without independent trials or deployment. Their source findings remain valid at their original scope, not as observed live failure rates.

Note18 explains another responsibility: preserving and checking selected records. Strict envelope/hash checks, disk-reread append locks, no-live-fallback replay, supplied-witness comparisons and conflict-refusing grouping are useful. They do not imply truthful/complete history, independent data, restored effects or authenticated downstream acceptance. A valid receipt over the example's refused candidate is consistent with this intended scope, not a promotion. Test sources include child contention, I/O injection and real cryptographic verification but were not run here.

Q04g1c/Q04g2b/Q06d1b/Q06d2b keep stronger uncertainty without turning every possible live campaign into a prerequisite for bounded source closure. New caller/source/version/schema/configuration/host/authority evidence can reopen dependent findings. The separate deterministic audit script, broader workflows/orchestration, hosts and release evidence remain distinct frontiers.
