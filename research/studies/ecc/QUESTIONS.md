## Preserved rivals and limits

Host behaviour must not be credited to ECC merely because a workflow names it. Shared source does not establish equivalent invocation. A clean synthesis may hide mixed product maturity. A successful cache checker may cover only selected fields. Method detail does not itself prevent omission. The local falsifier is decisive only for the narrow all-current-reference existence inference it actually challenges.
