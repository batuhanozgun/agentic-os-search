## Competing readings

An unreviewed vault is not necessarily a missing approval feature: the design deliberately separates governed acceptance. Rejecting an otherwise found ID due to another invalid document is an intentional completeness/uniqueness tradeoff. Partial search remains useful when diagnostics are carried. JSON/MCP do not share every terminal omission. Core storage accepting an unreviewed assertion is not a promise of factual correctness. Example source matching can be useful without establishing truth or native-harness integration.

Prior alternatives remain: flexible cards are not execution leases; real provisioning is not task acceptance; created-resource rollback is not restoration; identical content is not ownership; source refresh and historical views have distinct roles. One path's absence does not establish global absence.
