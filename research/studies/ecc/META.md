ECC combines reusable operating guidance and deployable host adaptations with several real work, execution, information, review and control mechanisms. Particular code/data connections exist, but there is no single uniform task/session/authority/measurement/acceptance contract across the examined arrangements. This is an explanatory assembly, not a final architecture or a claim about every possible custom integration.

Important positive paths include managed materialization/ownership, Rust process→SQLite→TUI observation, effectful TUI activation and stopping, persistent remote-routing outcomes, Canvas feedback, instinct-context injection, transactional candidate-registry updates, cooperative capsule append and same-archive release qualification.
