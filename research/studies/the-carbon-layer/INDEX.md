## File roles

`STATE.md` owns collection work/authorization; `META.md` owns classification/reuse; `VIDEO-CATALOG.md` is derived discovery; source and research files carry evidence at their declared extent. A linked external package keeps its own work authority.

For a new video, use `videos/<exact-video-id>-<stable-short-topic>/`. Preserve the case-sensitive source ID. Publication order and research order are separate catalog fields; do not encode transient rank or completion into the directory name.

A new substantive video unit normally grows META, STATE, SOURCE, RESEARCH and SOUL-DEVOS-ASSESSMENT surfaces, with evidence/notes as needed. Those are future shapes, not files asserted to exist already. Preserve their semantics while allowing coherent larger studies to use a richer local plan/router.

The existing `mQfTdNVCOB0` folder deliberately contains only metadata and a research route. Its results remain in [harness-engineering-and-evolution](../harness-engineering-and-evolution/INDEX.md). Do not introduce a local duplicate STATE, copy its findings, or count it twice as independent evidence.

The collection is discoverable through the parent [studies INDEX](../INDEX.md) and [CATALOG](../CATALOG.md); neither replaces detailed video research. Root research routing remains unchanged.
