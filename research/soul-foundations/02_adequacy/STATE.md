- H1: the four regions may be sufficient for Development OS while still being incomplete for general-purpose SOUL.

- H4: purpose/goals, governance/authority, verification/evidence and adaptation may either reduce cleanly into the four regions + relations or expose a missing first-order region; this must be tested rather than assumed.
