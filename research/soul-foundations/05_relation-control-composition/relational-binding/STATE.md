## Protected question

> Which accepted-ground anchors are connected, in what typed role, direction, scope, reference frame and time, and what semantic difference would be lost by replacing that binding with a generic link?

## Research object

Relational Binding studies cross-ground **connection semantics** among accepted Foundation anchors.

It does not assume:
- pairwise edges are always sufficient;
- every binding is binary;
- relation names are universal primitives;
- graph/database representation defines the semantics;
- represented/asserted bindings are automatically true or currently valid;
- derived standings, controls or transitions belong inside Binding merely because they can be encoded as relations.

## Primary falsification questions

- What minimum semantic information is required for a binding to be decision-usable rather than a generic `related-to` link?
- Are endpoint/anchor identity, role, direction/order, cardinality/arity, scope/reference frame, time/validity and qualification all independent load-bearing dimensions?
- When is a binding inherently n-ary or qualified rather than safely decomposable into binary links?
- Which recurrent binding families are stable across grounds/domains: representation/reference, participation/role, dependency/support, provenance/attribution, target/reference, access/situation or others?
- Do any such families deserve immediate child passes, or are they cross-cutting semantic families under one bounded Binding node?
- How must actual binding be separated from an informational assertion/record that a binding exists?
- Where does Binding end and Composite Standing, Control or Transition/Effect begin?
- Does Binding research expose an upstream composition-parent or four-ground adequacy falsifier?
