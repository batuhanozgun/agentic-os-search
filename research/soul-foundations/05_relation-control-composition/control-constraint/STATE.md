## Protected question

> What makes a candidate action, transition, decision or continuation permitted, required, prohibited, blocked, gated, conditioned, escalated or otherwise governed, and which distinct semantics must be preserved among norm/policy, legitimate authority, deontic standing, decision, enforcement, monitoring/compliance and alternate-path closure?

## Research object

Control / Constraint studies cross-ground governance and intervention semantics that cannot be reduced to:
- a policy document;
- an Actor's local authority standing;
- one Composite Standing result;
- technical reachability;
- one Work gate/status;
- or a later transition history.

Candidate pressure includes:
- permission / prohibition / obligation;
- authorization / approval / denial / waiver;
- gates, preconditions and escalation;
- authority, delegation and scope;
- advisory versus mechanically enforced controls;
- runtime/sandbox/network/tool restrictions;
- alternate-path and transitive effect-power closure;
- monitoring/compliance/violation signals;
- separation of duties / independent approval;
- fail-open/fail-closed and exception handling where semantically material.

These are probes, not accepted children.

## Primary falsification questions

- Is Control / Constraint a coherent composition region distinct from Binding, Standing and Transition?
- Can policy/norm, authority, deontic modality, decision/gate, enforcement and compliance be collapsed without losing decision-critical failure modes?
- Which of these are universal control-semantic layers versus optional/domain-specific profiles?
- Is `authorization` a standing, a control decision, an enforcement outcome or several distinct things depending on question?
- What makes a human approval or automated gate semantically valid rather than merely present in workflow topology?
- When does technical inability/blocking constitute control, and when is it merely Environment condition?
- How should alternate paths, delegated actors/tools and transitive effect power affect control adequacy without equating reachability with legitimate authority?
- Does control require path/coverage semantics beyond local permit/deny decisions?
- What distinguishes compliance standing/observation from control rule and enforcement?
- Do normative, decisional and technical control families deserve immediate children, or do they overlap too strongly?
- Does the pass expose a missing Binding/Standing distinction, a Transition dependency, or an upstream parent/four-ground falsifier?
