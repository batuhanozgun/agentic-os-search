## Protected question

> What criterion-, target-, use-, authority-, evidence-, reference-frame- or time-relative standing holds only when several accepted-ground facts and bindings are jointly evaluated, and what exact anchors make that standing valid, current and decision-usable?

## Research object

Composite Standing / Qualification studies **multi-anchor evaluative or derived standings** whose correctness cannot be safely localized as one intrinsic field on Environment, Information, Work or Actors.

Candidate examples include:
- actionable now;
- ready / supported / blocked relative to method and conditions;
- technically possible;
- authorized for a specific operation/context;
- verified against an exact target/requirement/version;
- validated / fit for intended use;
- accepted by an authorized party/forum;
- goal/outcome attained;
- evidence sufficient for a claim/decision;
- source authoritative / reliable enough for a particular use;
- compliant / current / relevant / eligible under explicit criteria.

These examples are probes, not accepted children.

## Primary falsification questions

- Does Composite Standing remain distinct from Relational Binding, Control and Transition after deeper research?
- What minimum semantic signature makes a standing meaningful rather than a generic status label?
- Which anchors are universal and which are family-specific: subject/target, criterion, evidence, evaluator/authority, use/purpose, reference frame, time/currentness, method/procedure, Environment conditions?
- Is `standing` one coherent region, or do readiness/actionability, assurance, authority/eligibility, outcome/attainment, epistemic sufficiency or other families deserve separate child passes?
- Can a standing be binary/boolean by default, or must degree, threshold, uncertainty, partial satisfaction and unknown/indeterminate states be first-class where material?
- What distinguishes actual standing, evaluated standing, asserted/recorded status and authorized judgment?
- What invalidates a prior standing when target/version, criterion, evidence, authority, environment condition or time changes?
- When is a standing intrinsic/local rather than composite, and how do we avoid moving ordinary ground-local conditions into this child?
- Does standing composition expose an upstream Binding, parent-composition or four-ground adequacy falsifier?
