## Current S1 hypothesis

A composite standing appears to require two layers that must not be collapsed:

```text
constitutive / truth basis
!=
evaluation / access basis
```

The constitutive basis concerns what facts/bindings/criteria make the standing hold. The evaluation/access basis concerns what evidence, method, model, assessor or decision rule is used to know/estimate/adjudicate it.

An evaluation may be wrong; in institutional cases an authorized act may instead be constitutive of the standing. S1 must refine this without forcing one metaphysical model across domains.
