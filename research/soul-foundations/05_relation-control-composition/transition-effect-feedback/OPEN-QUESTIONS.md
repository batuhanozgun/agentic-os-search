## Parent reopen triggers

Reopen the Transition parent if:
- accepted Transition / Propagation later fails its ground-local boundary;
- accepted Actual Causation later fails its episode/configuration boundary;
- Contrastive Causal Effect cannot be bounded without becoming general statistics/epistemology or a new composition sibling;
- Causation becomes independent of effect/change semantics;
- Feedback/Adaptation cannot be bounded without duplicating Control, Work or Domain Dynamics;
- Evolution or another candidate develops an independent stable protected question;
- independent replication identifies a superior structure.

## Non-reopen conditions

Do not reopen merely because:
- a domain uses a different causal formalism;
- one causal episode has many contributors;
- actual causation uses mishap reconstruction while contrastive effect uses experiments/statistics;
- feedback is delayed/nonlinear;
- later architecture uses causal graphs, fault trees, event buses or learning systems.
