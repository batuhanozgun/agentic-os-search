# Actors Ground Audit — FINDINGS MAP

**Document role:** CURRENT_L1  
**Status:** bounded audit result / awaiting user evaluation  
**Date:** 2026-09-05

## 1. Does Actors survive as a distinct Foundation ground?

Yes.

Actors cannot be reduced to Environment, Information or Work without losing bearer/role continuity, participation, authority/obligation, representation/delegation, attribution/accountability and collective/non-human agency semantics.

Route: `A5-ADVERSARIAL-RIVAL-REDUCTION-AUDIT.md`.

## 2. Did the prior Actors corpus remain current enough to audit?

Yes. Its preferred current surfaces were recovered through the exact Actors-local freshness manifest without currentness conflict.

Route: `A0-EVIDENCE-RECOVERY.md`.

## 3. What changed under the four-ground boundary?

The corpus remains valid, but several anti-collapse distinctions become sharper:

```text
Actor standing != representation/evidence of standing
Actor capability/competence != Environment actionability
Actor authority != technical effect power/runtime enforcement
Actor participation/assignment != Work identity/admission/execution
Work requiredness != Actor obligation
Work acceptance state != authority to accept
```

Route: `A1-FOUR-GROUND-BOUNDARY-COVERAGE.md`.

## 4. Is effective/transitive authority a missing Actors family?

No.

A reachable effect path can exist without legitimate authority. The case combines Actor authority/delegation, Environment reachability/capability, Work/orchestration path, policy/enforcement and Information evidence.

It is a high-priority later relation/control problem, not a new Actors child.

Routes: A1, `A2-FIRST-ORDER-GAP-TEST.md`, A3, A5.

## 5. Is epistemic authority / trust / expertise missing?

No new Actors-local family is forced.

Expertise/competence and mandate/certification roles are Actor-side; source/provenance/reliability/evidence standing is Information-side; review/certification is Work.

Route: A2.

## 6. Do beneficiaries / affected parties / rights holders break `Actors`?

They break a literal `Actor = active doer` interpretation, but that interpretation was already rejected.

These remain semantically relevant bearer/stake/participation positions even without positive action. No independent Parties/Stakeholders ground is currently justified.

Routes: A2, A5.

## 7. Is stewardship / standing responsibility missing?

No.

It maps to bearer + role/office + obligation + context/time + accountability/forum, with Work generated when action becomes due.

Important rule:

```text
standing Actor responsibility != current Work episode
```

Route: A2.

## 8. Do modern AI agents require a separate non-human ground?

No.

Greater operational discretion strengthens the need to separate operational agency from authority/obligation, institutional/legal attribution, accountability and personhood; it does not collapse them.

Routes: A2, A3, A5.

## 9. Do multi-agent systems expose a new Actor family?

No.

Hierarchy, subagents, generator/reviewer/verifier roles, human gates and orchestration topologies instantiate existing bearer/role/participation/delegation/authority/attribution distinctions.

Critical rules:

```text
hierarchy position != authority != competence != accountability
multi-agent team != automatically a corporate/group Actor
```

Route: `A3-NEW-CANDIDATE-STUDY-HOLDOUTS.md`.

## 10. Why was broad new external search skipped?

A4 was conditional. After A1–A3, no live first-order gap hypothesis remained; the prior Actors corpus already spans materially different evidence traditions. A5 did not revive a gap.

Route: `A4-EXTERNAL-HOLDOUT-DECISION.md`.

## 11. What internal Actors structure is retained?

The prior multi-anchor distinctions remain valuable as **overlapping analytical dimensions**, not children:

1. bearer identity / continuity;
2. role / office / position / membership;
3. situation / episode / context / time;
4. participation and decision roles;
5. capability / competence;
6. permission / authority / obligation;
7. representation / delegation / impersonation;
8. action / decision / omission;
9. attribution;
10. responsibility / accountability;
11. shared / collective / corporate agency;
12. non-human operational participation vs normative attribution.

Non-performing affected/beneficiary/rights-holder positions are explicit orientation additions, not a new child.

## 12. Immediate-child judgment

No stable universal immediate-child split survived.

Rejected as universal child decompositions:

- Standing / Participation;
- Bearer & Identity / Authority & Representation / Participation & Agency / Attribution & Responsibility;
- Individual / Collective / Non-human;
- Operational Agency / Normative Standing;
- Identity/Standing State / Agency/Participation Dynamics.

Current preferred structural result:

> **Actors is a boundedly sufficient ground with a rich relational multi-anchor map and no promoted immediate-child hierarchy at current Foundation depth.**

Route: A5.

## 13. Naming judgment

Keep `Actors / Agency–Participation–Authority` for now, with an explicit warning that `Actors` is an umbrella label and does not mean only active doers or one Actor primitive.

Possible naming refinement can be revisited during top-level adequacy.
