## Protected Actors formulation

> Actors / Agency–Participation–Authority concerns which persistent bearers/positions/parties are implicated in a situation, in what capacity and standing, through which agency/participation/representation relations, and how authority/obligation, action/non-action, attribution/responsibility and collective/non-human agency are distributed across context and time.

This is a research-ground formulation, not an ontology primitive or architecture component.
