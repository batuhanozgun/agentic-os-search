# Information / Representation State — Deferred Relations

**Status:** active ledger; relation capture only, not composition research

Record cross-ground relations encountered while defining Information. Do not solve a general typed relation/control model here.

## Initial deferred relations

### Environment ↔ Information

- actual state / event / condition ↔ observation or representation about it;
- measurement/observation may be partial, delayed, noisy or wrong while actual reality remains distinct;
- freshness/as-of binds representation to temporal claims about reality.

### Work ↔ Information

- Work may consume, produce, transform, select or preserve representations;
- goal/decision/commitment semantics must not be reduced to information merely because they can be represented;
- current context is often selected relative to current Work.

### Actors ↔ Information

- Actors may observe, assert, believe, infer, hold, share, authorize or dispute representations;
- Actor-local belief/model state and the represented informational content require a boundary test;
- authority over an assertion/source is not the same as factual truth.

### Information ↔ Records / persistence

- persistent use introduces additional identity, preservation, history, retrieval and disposition obligations;
- persistence does not establish authority/truth/evidence sufficiency;
- recordhood may depend on role/purpose rather than persistence alone.

### Information ↔ technical context/memory mechanisms

- retrieval, graph representation, context selection and memory lifecycle are mechanisms/engineering concerns that act on informational states;
- mechanism classification must not be imported as Foundation hierarchy.

## Guardrail

Only relations necessary to define the Information boundary may become load-bearing in this node pass. Systematic relation/control composition remains deferred.
