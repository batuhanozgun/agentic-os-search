## Accepted result

Formation is one source-/reference-relative informational inquiry with no immediate children. Target reality, immediate source and formed representation must remain distinguishable. Faithful capture of an assertion does not establish the assertion's referent truth. New token, receiver copy, source episode, content and independent evidence are different kinds of novelty.

Acquisition can be model-mediated and interleaved with inference; it need not be raw or passive. Coverage of captured records does not establish coverage of all relevant sources. Missing/null/unknown is not measured zero without a source contract. Reference time is not receipt time. A correctly acquired forecast is not observation of its future target.

F2's record-only limitation states that an exact answer is recoverable from a record only when the still-allowed situations producing that record agree on the answer. It does not prohibit useful reasoning, justified additional assumptions or new evidence. No real system's observation model was established by the finite example.

The strongest source-contact/coverage versus realization rival remains explicit. Leaf status preserves those facets rather than turning acquisition into a single success flag.
