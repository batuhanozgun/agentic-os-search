## Shortest result

Re-expression is one interpreted source-to-derivative inquiry. Ask what required meaning, relation, uncertainty or distinction survives under the intended interpretation and use, what loss is legitimate, and what evidence actually supports the claim. No immediate child is promoted.

## Most important refinement

Information being recoverable by some decoder does not establish that a target expression means the right thing to its intended recipient. Actual successful use is a further question. Two inverse wrong label mappings can pass a round trip while the intermediate meaning is wrong.

```
existential recoverability != intended semantic fidelity != successful use
source-supported output != sufficient output for every purpose
same words != same roles, conditions or order
small value error != stable threshold decision
local success != compatible composition
output-only verification != complete source/task coverage
```

Legitimate abstraction need not be lossless. Uncertainty and unresolved alternatives may need preservation rather than forced resolution. Codebooks, versions, units, recipient knowledge and recoverable sources can be part of an adequate interpretation; a standalone summary cannot claim what only its source link supplies.
