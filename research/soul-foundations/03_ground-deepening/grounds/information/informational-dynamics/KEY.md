# Informational Dynamics — KEY

**Updated:** 2026-09-12  
**Role:** CURRENT_L0.

Formation asks how a source/origin becomes an informational representation. Derivation asks how existing information is re-expressed, used to warrant a conclusion or revised as a continuing state. Selection asks which existing information reaches a use/consumer. These are protected questions, not an obligatory order.

Formation's leaf/F6 and Derivation's three-child parent/T6 are accepted. Re-expression is the first Derivation child with a dedicated result and now proposes a bounded leaf, pending evaluation. Inferential Construction, Maintained-Information Revision and Selection still have no dedicated passes and are inactive.

Accepted boundaries include source fidelity versus truth, lineage versus warrant, entailed output versus purpose-sufficiency, and proposed revision versus actual application. The new transfer R6 proposal distinguishes possible decoding from intended meaning and successful use, and qualifies roundtrip checks, permitted loss, decision margins and verifier coverage. These refinements await evaluation, not automatic parent adoption.

Records, communication, propagation, uncertainty, time and lineage remain profiles/interfaces or cross-cutting questions. Current STATE controls authorization. Same-model review and synthetic/native checks do not independently validate the tree or live SOUL behavior.
