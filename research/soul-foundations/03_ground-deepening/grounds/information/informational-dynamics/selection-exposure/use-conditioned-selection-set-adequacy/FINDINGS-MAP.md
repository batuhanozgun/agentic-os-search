# Use-conditioned selection — Question-to-evidence map

**Date:** 2026-09-15. **Status:** U0–U3 checkpoint; no final structural result.
**Use:** find a relevant research claim and then read its conditions, not a substitute for evidence or a SOUL schema.

| Reuse question / trigger | Bounded finding and applicability | Exact evidence | What is not established |
|---|---|---|---|
| Must the selected view identify every fact before a choice is possible? | No for a specified finite decision contract: every indistinguishability cell needs a common optimal action.
