# Use-conditioned selection — Residuals and reopening

**Date:** 2026-09-15. Proposed bounded leaf and U5.1–U5.6 await evaluation.

## Structural reversal conditions

The strongest rival remains need/adequacy specification versus constrained view construction. Reopen if recurring criterion-discovery work has a stable Selection-local protected outcome that the explicit facet routes hide, rather than merely reauthorizing Work or validating a claim. U4's leaf judgment is not a universal prohibition on such a child.

Reopen if adaptive inquiry exposes an informational outcome not preserved by qualified selection modes and neighboring Formation/Derivation/Work questions; if explanation or exploration must be distorted into a fixed action objective; if the map repeatedly loses a decisive use/quantifier/constraint; or if independent review finds a better lower-cost structure. Different algorithms, a new use profile or more successful examples do not alone decide the hierarchy.

## Evidence and semantic unknowns

Real uses need defensible criteria, state/candidate boundaries, source interpretation, admissibility and loss/horizon conditions. We have not supplied a general natural-language sufficiency oracle. User agreement on a research result does not certify that the same assumptions hold in a later SOUL decision.

The original K01–K06 are finite model cases. New N01 checks exact recorded rationale, not true causation or introspective faithfulness; N02 is a small SQL witness example, not independent empirical support or general provenance correctness; N03 covers a supplied finite function class, not production completeness. The generalized acceptable-output condition in U5 has an explicit finite proof but no broad semantic benchmark. More than one policy/risk criterion may be defensible; none was universally adopted.

P4–P6 source depth and discovery failures are in U4-SOURCE-AND-EXECUTION-ADDENDUM; original P1–P3 limits remain in SOURCE-LEDGER. No whole-paper-proof replication, independent researcher/model validation, live actor behavior or calibrated real workload was obtained. These are residuals, not automatically authorized research queues.

## Deferred boundaries

Work/Actors/Control retain goal authorization, decision rights, responsibilities and actual activities. Informational adequacy findings can inform later actor evaluation, but they are not installed behavior or proof of competence/compliance. Warrant and causal inquiry retain whether the offered reasons establish the claim or actual influence. Selecting witnesses does not settle those questions.

Exposure retains whether specified information is actually available to the intended consumer at the required endpoint. Re-expression retains meaning transfer and interpretation resources. Revision retains continuing-state change; Records retains future recoverability and disposition. A present-use selection verdict does not authorize deletion of unselected research or guarantee indefinite future utility.
