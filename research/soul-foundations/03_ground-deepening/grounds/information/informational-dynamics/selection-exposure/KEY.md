Selection asks which existing resources, parts or permitted contingent views serve the inquiry's need. Exposure asks which specified information is available for a consumer under the required endpoint and mode. These are not two software services or a universal choose-then-send sequence.

```text
information need != query proxy
source universe != index != eligible candidates != exposed view

resource address / offsets != exact representation / fragment identity
individually newest != jointly coherent or appropriate as-of
chosen IDs != content present in the required invocation
planned bundle != budgeted exposed bundle
availability != demonstrated interpretation, acceptance or action
```
