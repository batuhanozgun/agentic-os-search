# Exposure — Residuals and reopening conditions

**Date:** 2026-09-15. Proposed leaf/E5.1–E5.5; not accepted merely by publication.

## Structural questions

Reopen the opportunity/presentation split if direct facet routing repeatedly hides a stable first-order inquiry, or if a distinct access-provisioning/presentation branch lowers material diagnostic loss without importing a mandatory pipeline. The current leaf has a richer contract and can be summarized badly; it is not evidence that those failures are trivial.

Reopen the parent boundary if Exposure reduces wholly to Formation or Propagation with no information-local loss, or if preserving consumer-supported form requires annexing all interpretation/cognition. A new human-facing or non-network case that cannot be represented without distortion would be material. Different tools, modes or green tests alone do not establish a child.

## Actual access, evidence and time

Real uses must establish the intended resource/version, consumer, locus, permitted action family, cost/deadline, capability/credential state and representation dependencies. X01 supplies these facts in a tiny strict-time model; it does not discover them for an actual environment. Non-deterministic/stochastic availability, scheduling competition and bounded guarantees require their own models and evidence.

The difference between per-world path existence and an actor-executable policy remains important. E2's two-location case is analytical, not an executed access planner. Actual availability, known availability and a guarantee under uncertainty are not identical. No universally appropriate risk threshold or strategy was selected.

One endpoint observation does not certify all applicable consumers, future reads or dependency stability. Receipts may support more or less depending on what actually generated them. The local suite does not test response loss, malicious reporting, race conditions, distributed consensus or exhaustive protocol conformance.

## Representation, custody and use

A decoder or presentation path may be available yet semantically wrong; a semantically valid one may be unavailable. Neither X05 nor WCAG source reading establishes human/model understanding. P6 supplies historical communication pressure, not measured current actor behavior or a required grounding procedure.

A route restriction need not remove copies, embeddings, recollections or later derived views; the performed fixture only shows one ordinary file remains. That observation does not grant use authority or demonstrate global erasure. Reliance withdrawal, copy disposition and access policy retain Records/Revision/Control owners. Unknown copy scope limits claims of total removal.

Metadata may preserve a legitimate discovery record while underlying evidence is unavailable. Future research reuse may therefore need fresh source recovery, revalidation or an explicit evidence gap. No requirement to retain every source forever or disclose protected information follows.

The source ledger and E4 retain uneven reading depths and access failures. The six cases and same-fixture controls, two unchanged runs and file identities do not constitute independent science or live-system efficacy. Original predecessor tests were not rerun.
