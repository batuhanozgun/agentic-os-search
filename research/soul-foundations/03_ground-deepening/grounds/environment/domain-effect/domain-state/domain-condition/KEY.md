### Child 1 — Realized Characteristics / Values

Primary question:

> **What materially relevant characteristics, quantities, qualitative values, modes or relational conditions actually obtain at the selected scope and time, before asking whether they satisfy a separate criterion?**

Examples:
- temperature, pressure, concentration, load, price, spread;
- wear/damage or other realized condition;
- current mode/regime such as Manual/Test/Running;
- current qualitative or relational condition.

### Child 2 — Criterion-Relative Standing / Status

Primary question:

> **Relative to a materially relevant functional, normative, regulatory or domain criterion, what standing currently obtains?**

Examples:
- healthy / degraded / failed;
- ready / not ready;
- compliant / non-compliant;
- valid / invalid;
- good standing / not in good standing;
- severity/quality standing relative to a criterion.

The criterion/rule itself is not automatically Domain Condition. The child protects the true current standing **relative to** that criterion.

## Why two children survive

The split changes recovery reasoning:
- current characteristics can be correct while the wrong/inapplicable criterion produces the wrong standing;
- a standing can sometimes be correctly established without a complete description of every underlying condition detail;
- materially different source traditions distinguish current properties/state/mode from health/quality/standing questions.

The split is semantic, not raw-vs-derived data, scalar-vs-enum or physical-vs-social.
