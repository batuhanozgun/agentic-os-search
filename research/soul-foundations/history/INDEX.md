# SOUL Foundations — History Archive

## Role

This directory preserves superseded research paths and point-in-time handoffs that remain useful for audit, reconstruction and later falsifier recovery but are **not current project authority**.

Current project state and authorization live only in:
- `../STATE.md`
- `../INDEX.md`

Historical files must not override those current surfaces merely because search/retrieval finds them directly.

## Handoffs

`handoffs/` preserves point-in-time transition records. They answer questions such as:
- what was authorised at a past checkpoint;
- why a research direction changed;
- what assumptions or hypotheses were carried into a later pass.

A handoff's internal `current` language is historical to the date/checkpoint recorded by that file unless the parent current router explicitly promotes it again.

## Superseded research

`superseded/operating-world-environment/` preserves the completed broad Environment research that studied a boundary-relative external operating-world problem rather than the intended Technical Work Environment problem.

That research may contain valid findings for its own question. It is retained because:
- the scope failure is decision-relevant audit evidence;
- later research may independently rediscover an operating-world problem;
- the corpus can then be recovered deliberately rather than reconstructed from memory.

It is **not** one of the four current intended foundation verticals and is not evidence for the Technical Work Environment result.

## Retrieval rule

Use history only deliberately. For current research orientation, begin from `../STATE.md` and `../INDEX.md`.
