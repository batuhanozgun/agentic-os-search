# Actors — Vertical Research

## Purpose

Research the actors problem domain independently before using it inside a broader SOUL architecture model.

The provisional target includes entities that perform actions, make or execute decisions, exercise capability or authority, accept responsibility, delegate, or are held accountable.

## Vertical isolation

Relations to records/artifacts, work, tools, environments, governance, or other domains may be recorded when material but should not redirect this vertical into those domains.

## Status

This vertical is intentionally not started yet. Its detailed research questions and internal package structure should be defined when this vertical becomes the authorised active research line, informed by but not mechanically copied from earlier vertical research.

## Epistemic note

`Actors` is a provisional research label, not an accepted SOUL primitive.
