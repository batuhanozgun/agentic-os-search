# Work — Vertical Research

## Purpose

Research the Work problem domain independently before using it inside a broader SOUL architecture model.

`Work` is a provisional research label, not an accepted SOUL primitive and not a pre-agreed final category. The research may refine, split, replace, or reject the starting frame if evidence warrants it.

A weak starting hypothesis is that the area includes phenomena around things that must be done, changed, investigated, decided, verified, recovered, coordinated, or completed, together with the conditions under which such activity is represented, constrained, controlled, evaluated, and brought to closure. This is a research prompt, not a definition.

## Vertical isolation

Relations to records/artifacts, actors, authority, tools, or other domains may be recorded when material but should not redirect this vertical into those domains.

Isolation is methodological rather than ontological. The research must not assume that Work is truly separable in reality.

Records substantive findings are not Work axioms. Records material should be reopened only when a concrete Work question requires it.

## Research discipline

The vertical should:

- separate established context, working hypotheses, open questions, and decision-critical assumptions;
- test the initial frame against materially different units of analysis and disciplinary traditions;
- allow evidence to change the research direction;
- distinguish exploratory findings from current conclusions;
- trace load-bearing claims back to primary sources where feasible;
- use adversarial / omission checks without presenting same-model self-review as independent verification;
- stop when additional search for first-order distinctions has materially diminishing information value and major omissions have been challenged.

## Status

This vertical is active and explicitly authorised as of 2026-09-01, following evaluation of the completed Records vertical.

The internal research package structure should emerge from the Work evidence rather than being mechanically copied from Records.

## Boundary

This authorisation covers independent Work foundations research and necessary Work-state/retrieval maintenance. It does not authorise Actors, cross-vertical synthesis, SOUL architecture, or implementation design.
