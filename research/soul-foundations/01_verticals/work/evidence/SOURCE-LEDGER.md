# Work — Source Ledger

## Role

Claim-to-source recovery ledger for the Work vertical.

This ledger distinguishes the **source itself** from the **view actually inspected**. A publisher abstract, search snippet or derived page is not treated as equivalent to reading a primary full text. Load-bearing claims prefer full primary/authoritative material where feasible; where only an abstract was needed/available, the claim is bounded to what that abstract directly supports.

### View grades

- **A — primary/authoritative full or normative view inspected:** full paper/specification/handbook content or an authoritative standard page sufficient for the cited claim.
- **B — primary publication abstract / institutional publication record inspected:** suitable for claims stated directly in the abstract; not treated as full-paper verification.
- **C — bibliographic or publisher summary only:** contextual support only; not used as sole load-bearing evidence.

## Sources

### S01 — OMG CMMN overview

- **Source:** Object Management Group, *Case Management Model and Notation (CMMN)*.
- **URL:** https://www.omg.org/cmmn/
- **Type:** authoritative standards-body overview.
- **View grade:** A for overview claims.
- **Supports:** cases with activities in unpredictable order; evolving situations; event-centered/adaptive case work; complementarity with BPMN.
- **Used in:** orientation; units; situatedness/control findings.

### S02 — OMG CMMN 1.0 specification

- **Source:** Object Management Group, *Case Management Model and Notation, Version 1.0*, formal/2014-05-05.
- **URL:** https://www.omg.org/spec/CMMN/1.0/PDF
- **Type:** normative specification.
- **View grade:** A; execution-semantics, PlanningTable/DiscretionaryItem, applicability and Stage/Task lifecycle sections directly inspected, including rendered page views.
- **Supports:** discretionary potential work versus items selected into a runtime plan; applicability; Available/Enabled/Disabled/Active/Suspended/Failed/Completed/Terminated distinctions; transitions; sentries/entry-exit criteria; requiredness/manual activation/repetition controls.
- **Used in:** units; coordination; state/closure/recovery; intake/commitment findings.
- **Scope caution:** CMMN concepts are evidence that these distinctions can matter; they are not asserted as a universal SOUL case/work model.

### S10 — Little's Law

- **Source:** John D. C. Little (1961), *A Proof for the Queuing Formula: L = λW*, Operations Research 9(3), 383–387.
- **DOI:** 10.1287/opre.9.3.383
- **URL:** https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/opre.9.3.383
- **Type:** peer-reviewed primary paper.
- **View grade:** B/A for the theorem statement; publisher page gives the original abstract, assumptions and formula directly.
- **Supports:** under stated stationarity/finite-mean assumptions, relationship among mean number in system, arrival rate and mean time in system.
- **Used in:** orientation; units; flow findings.
- **Scope caution:** the theorem does not itself prescribe WIP limits, prioritization or management policy.

### S11 — Sagas / compensation in long-running work

- **Source:** Hector Garcia-Molina & Kenneth Salem (1987), *Sagas*, SIGMOD '87, 249–259.
- **DOI:** 10.1145/38713.38742
- **Primary PDF:** https://www.cs.cornell.edu/andru/cs711/2002fa/reading/sagas.pdf
- **ACM metadata:** https://doi.org/10.1145/38713.38742
- **Type:** primary peer-reviewed conference paper.
- **View grade:** A; full PDF text plus rendered pages directly inspected.
- **Supports:** long-running units decomposed into interleavable transactions; compensating transactions for partial execution; compensation is semantic and need not restore exact prior database state; concurrent/parallel recovery considerations.
- **Used in:** state/closure/recovery findings.
- **Scope caution:** database transaction semantics are used as a counterexample showing recovery distinctions, not as a general Work implementation model.

- **Source:** ISO Online Browsing Platform, ISO/TR 12489 maintenance terms citing ISO 14224 / IEC reliability terminology.
- **URL:** https://www.iso.org/obp/ui?_escaped_fragment_=iso%3Astd%3Aiso%3Atr%3A12489%3Aed-1%3Av1%3Aen

## Evidence limits

- This is interdisciplinary evidence, not a statistical meta-analysis of all Work research.
- Some foundational sources are old because the relevant distinctions originate in mature fields; age alone does not invalidate a stable conceptual counterexample.
- Several claims rely on publisher/institutional abstracts rather than full texts. Those claims are deliberately bounded to abstract-level statements.
- The research uses standards and formal models as **existence proofs for distinctions**, not as evidence that SOUL should implement those standards.
- Older full-text professional standards are used only for stable conceptual distinctions where the current-edition status is explicitly noted; they are not treated as current compliance requirements.
- No same-model adversarial review in this vertical is described as independent verification.
