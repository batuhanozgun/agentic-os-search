## Currentness rule

The synthesis and `STATE.md` carry the current conclusion. Orientation and findings files preserve the research path and evidence decomposition; they should not be mistaken for a later current verdict if they conflict with the synthesis.

## Vertical boundary

Cross-relations to Records or Actors may appear as deferred questions only. Nothing in this index authorises cross-vertical synthesis, Actors research, SOUL architecture or implementation design.
