# Records / Artifacts — STATE

**Status:** active research  
**Parent:** `research/soul-foundations/`  

## Current objective

Establish a first-principles, source-grounded understanding of the records / artifacts problem domain before relating it to Work, Actors, the SOUL Development System, or SOUL architecture.

## Current research pass

Foundational pass: determine what a record is, what must persist for later trustworthy use, and which distinctions among data, information, document, record, state, event, claim, evidence, knowledge, memory, and derived representation are load-bearing.

This pass is explicitly technology-neutral. Storage engines, vector databases, filesystems, Git, knowledge graphs, and agent-memory mechanisms may appear as comparative evidence, but are not assumed to define the problem.

## Current starting frame

Investigate across adjacent disciplines rather than one inherited software frame:

- archival science / records management,
- information and data management,
- databases and temporal/event models,
- provenance and reproducibility,
- digital preservation,
- information retrieval and knowledge organisation,
- version/configuration management where it clarifies identity and change.

The frame is provisional and may be changed by research evidence.
