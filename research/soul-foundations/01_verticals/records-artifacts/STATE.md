# Records / Artifacts — STATE

**Status:** ready to research  
**Parent:** `research/soul-foundations/`  

## Current objective

Establish a first-principles, source-grounded understanding of the records / artifacts problem domain before relating it to Work, Actors, the SOUL Development System, or SOUL architecture.

## Current starting frame

Begin broadly with the nature and lifecycle of persistent information:

- definition and category boundaries,
- representation and persistence,
- storage models,
- organisation and relationships,
- identity and versioning,
- provenance and authority,
- change/correction/deletion,
- indexing/search/retrieval,
- reconstruction and context,
- failure modes and recovery.

The frame is provisional and may be changed by research evidence.

Start research with the foundational question: **What is a record, independently of a particular environment, storage medium, software architecture, or agent system?** Use adjacent disciplines and competing definitions to determine which distinctions are load-bearing before choosing a storage or knowledge-management model.
