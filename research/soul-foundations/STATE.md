## Technical Work Environment checkpoint

The replacement independent vertical was researched from the domain question:

> **What is a Work Environment / Çalışma Ortamı?**

It was deliberately not defined from Codex, GitHub, Development OS, SOUL, Records, Work or Actors.

Current project research label:

> **Technical Work Environment / Teknik Çalışma Ortamı**

The term is provisional/project-local; the research did not find one established domain-general label that precisely matches the intended technical region.

### Strongest bounded result

> **A Technical Work Environment is a bounded realized technical support context in which a selected set of capabilities, services, resources and interfaces are effectively available under particular constraints, configuration and current state. It may, but need not, have an explicit profile/definition. Where profiles exist, they are separable descriptive/prescriptive representations whose resolution may yield one or more concrete realizations. The environment may be local, remote, distributed, nested, shared, isolated, federated, ephemeral or dynamically resolved.**

### Material late correction

The research initially leaned toward a profile-first model. The omission challenge falsified that requirement.

Current rule:

> **Realized technical support context is primary for environment existence; an explicit profile/manifest is optional. Declared, observed/discovered and actual state must remain separable.**

### Current bounded dimension families

1. support boundary and scope;
2. composition and environment identity/equivalence;
3. technical configuration, resolution and realized state;
4. capabilities, services and resources;
5. interfaces and mediation;
6. constraints, isolation and access/resource policy;
7. distribution, nesting, federation and sharedness;
8. substrate/ambient technical dependencies;
9. lifecycle, persistence and retention behavior;
10. current condition: readiness, availability, capacity and degradation;
11. observability/discovery versus actual state;
12. control, mutability and administration surface;
13. purpose/role profile;
14. reproducibility, portability and protected equivalence;
15. dynamic resolution/adaptation over time.

These are research dimensions, not ontology classes or schema fields.

### Null/reduction result

The evidence does not require one intrinsic object/substance called `Environment`. Later representation could be relational/compositional.

The environment problem itself survives because technical boundary, support surface, current state/dependencies, identity/equivalence, lifecycle, health/readiness/capacity and observability still require explicit semantics.

Working research conclusion:

> **Environment may be relational/compositional in representation while remaining a first-order research problem.**

This is not an architecture decision.

### Completion basis

The final omission challenge tested structurally different cases including air-gapped/offline environments, secrets/licenses, snapshot/restore, managed/opaque environments, dynamic discovery/configuration, self-adaptive resources, hardware/HIL/FPGA, heterogeneous edge, shared multi-tenant interference, partial failure/readiness, hidden dependencies/nondeterminism, purpose-labelled environments, provider substitution/migration, broad digital-workplace counterframes and headless environments.

One late probe materially corrected the frame; subsequent probes predominantly mapped to the corrected dimensions and did not open another first-order family.

**Completion judgment:** boundedly sufficient for current independent foundation purpose.

**Verification limit:** same-model adversarial self-review; not independent verification or universal completeness.
