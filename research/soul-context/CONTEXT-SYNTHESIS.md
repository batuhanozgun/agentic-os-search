This file closes the contextual reading line that began with `os-architect`, `soul` and `keel` and later used `keel-research` as bounded predecessor evidence.

The purpose of this line was not to decide how SOUL Research as a whole must be conducted. It was to understand, from first-party historical/project sources, **why this research project exists and what kind of system Batu appears to want research to inform.**

## Directly supported context

The following are supported directly by the inspected first-party sources rather than inferred only from similarity.

### 1. SOUL is a first-principles rebuild, not a KEEL folder migration

The current SOUL foundation describes a general-purpose agentic operating architecture that receives an intended outcome and establishes the controlled system required to pursue it.

SOUL explicitly rejects definition by current KEEL structure, one fixed workflow, one provider/framework, one storage technology or one task domain. Existing KEEL mechanisms are prior art/candidates, not default architecture.

### 2. The intended product is larger than a single answer-producing agent

The desired output is a controlled working system around the outcome: task-specific capability formation, bounded authority, state/context/knowledge handling, work lifecycle, evidence/verification, failure/recovery, observability and governed evolution may all be needed depending on the task.

Generality is expected to come from **forming an appropriate task-specific system under a stable control model**, not from forcing one permanent team/workflow onto every task.

### 3. Development discipline is deliberately established before product architecture

SOUL Phase 0 exists because long-running architecture work would otherwise reproduce the same failure classes the product is meant to control: dependence on chat/model memory, drifting state, producer self-certification, weak verification, hidden assumptions and ambiguous continuation.

`development/` and `system/` are deliberately separated. Product architecture and technology selection are postponed until the process for making those decisions is controlled.

### 4. Predecessor lessons are inputs, not architecture truth

SOUL's own evidence synthesis explicitly records predecessor sources at SHA level and separates:

- source-derived principles,
- SOUL-specific synthesis decisions.

This is strong evidence that the new architecture is intended to learn from KEEL/os-architect history without treating inherited mechanisms as privileged defaults.

### 5. Research/evidence is not supposed to auto-promote into product decisions

SOUL governance and predecessor research practice distinguish research/evidence from architecture decision authority.

Material findings can challenge a frame, reopen a question or support a later decision, but they do not automatically become backlog items, repairs, permanent roles, mechanisms or accepted architecture.

### 6. The historical problem grew above the level of isolated product bugs

The predecessor corpus increasingly raised problems such as:

- question/request being mistaken for the real objective,
- result/state surviving while purpose, responsibility or next work is lost,
- correlated reviewers sharing the same source/frame/model error,
- controls measuring form while missing value,
- lessons existing but not being invoked when needed,
- owner becoming hidden orchestrator/verifier,
- local reasoning being competent while system-level work selection or framing remains ownerless,
- repeated fixes being made inside an inherited frame without asking whether that frame/mechanism should exist.

This helps explain why a first-principles SOUL line is materially different from simply hardening KEEL further.

## Bounded answer to the context question

A defensible working answer to:

> "Batu araştırma yaptıktan sonra nasıl bir yapı kurmak istiyor?"

is:

**Research is intended to inform a general-purpose agentic operating architecture that can take an intended outcome, form the task-specific working system needed for that outcome, operate under explicit control/authority/state/evidence rules, make failure and uncertainty visible, verify material claims without producer self-certification, preserve continuity across fresh sessions, and evolve capabilities in a governed way — without hard-coding KEEL's current mechanisms or one domain workflow as the universal solution.**

This is a context reconstruction from the inspected repositories. It is not a final SOUL architecture specification and not a statement that every listed concern must become a separate component, role, service or protocol.

## What this contextual study suggests about why SOUL Research exists

The strongest synthesis is:

SOUL Research exists to create an evidence base **before architecture commitment** so that the next system is not merely a cleaned-up inheritance of KEEL, current tool affordances or one model's preferred workflow.

The research needs to help distinguish at least:

- recurring/general problem properties from predecessor-specific accidents,
- stable control invariants from task/domain-specific operating structure,
- observed failures from interpretations of those failures,
- useful prior art from causally necessary architecture,
- evidence from decision authority,
- failure prevention/verification claims from mechanisms that only look disciplined.

This explains the direction of the later local taxonomy work, but does not canonize that taxonomy.

## Important non-proofs / remaining uncertainty

This context study does **not** establish that:

- D1–D11 are complete or optimally partitioned,
- V1–V4 are the correct validation framework,
- the selected external holdouts prove agentic effectiveness or SOUL generality,
- predecessor diagnoses are causally complete,
- every SOUL non-negotiable is empirically optimal,
- the final system requires any specific role topology, workflow, database, model family, framework or tool,
- the project-wide research method should use the provisional stopping/package models created here.

It also does not claim that all potentially relevant predecessor history has been exhaustively read. The reading was intentionally selective and information-value driven.

## Why the context line can stop here

The original contextual objective is now sufficiently supported to pause because:

1. the four repositories have differentiated source roles and navigable reading ledgers;
2. the KEEL → SOUL transition has direct first-party evidence rather than only inferred similarity;
3. the reason Development OS precedes product architecture is explicit;
4. predecessor lessons versus SOUL-specific synthesis are explicitly separated in SOUL's own evidence;
5. research/evidence versus architecture-decision authority is explicit;
6. a bounded answer exists for what kind of system the later research is meant to inform;
7. the early taxonomy/method ideas are clearly quarantined as provisional `soul-context` work rather than silently becoming project governance.

Further reading of these repositories can still refine or falsify this synthesis, but there is no need to continue expanding the contextual taxonomy before the user explicitly moves the project to **how the research itself should be designed and conducted**.
